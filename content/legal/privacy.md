# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how {{company}} ("we", "us"), operator of {{brand}}, collects and uses personal data. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

## 1. What we collect

- **Mobile number**, when you sign in with a WhatsApp OTP.
- **Questions you ask** and the filters you use, so we can answer them and improve the Service.
- **Usage data**: how many questions you have asked, your plan, and when you signed in.
- **Technical data**: IP address, browser type and basic logs, used for security and to prevent abuse.
- **Interest in Pro**, if you tell us you are interested.
- **Payment details** (when paid plans launch) are collected by our payment gateway, not by us. We only receive a payment reference and status.

We do not ask for your PAN, bank account, or investment holdings.

## 2. Why we use it

- To sign you in and keep your account secure.
- To answer your questions and run the screener.
- To apply free and paid plan limits.
- To prevent fraud, abuse and automated scraping.
- To contact you about your account or, if you asked, about Pro.
- To meet legal obligations.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, given when you sign in, and for legitimate uses permitted by law. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 6). Withdrawing it means we can no longer provide a signed-in account.

## 3. Where your data is kept

Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers in India (Mumbai region). To understand a question, the question text is processed by an AI language model running on Google Cloud in India. Mobile numbers and email addresses are removed from the text first. Fund data and answers are produced by our own systems.

## 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Service, under contract:

- Google Cloud (hosting, database, AI processing);
- our WhatsApp messaging provider (to deliver OTPs);
- our payment gateway (when paid plans launch).

We may also disclose data when required by law or by a government authority.

## 5. How long we keep it

- OTP codes expire after 5 minutes.
- Account and usage data are kept while your account is active, and deleted within 90 days of an erasure request, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
- Security logs are kept for a limited period, typically 30 days.

## 6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

- ask what personal data we hold about you;
- ask us to correct or complete it;
- ask us to erase it, and withdraw your consent;
- nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity;
- raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use these rights, write to {{grievance_email}} from your registered details, or message {{grievance_phone}} on WhatsApp.

## 7. Cookies

We use only essential cookies: one to keep you signed in, and one to count free questions for visitors. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 8. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, signed session cookies and server-side limits to protect your data. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as required by law.

## 9. Children

The Service is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
